Who We Are

The ALMA–GEC Team

The ALMA GEC Team is a dedicated group of student leaders committed to serving, guiding, and uplifting members of the BYU Global Education Center (BYU GEC) community. United by shared values of faith, integrity, and service, the team works collaboratively to create a supportive environment where students can grow spiritually, academically, and socially.

Inspired by the teachings and leadership principles found in the Book of Alma, the team promotes righteous decision-making, unity, and Christlike service.

As leaders, the ALMA GEC Team plays a vital role in organizing activities, mentoring fellow students, and strengthening the overall student experience within the BYU GEC. Through meaningful programs, leadership opportunities, and service initiatives, the team helps BYU students feel connected, empowered, and inspired to reach their full potential.

With a strong commitment to excellence and discipleship, the ALMA GEC Team continues to be a guiding force in building a vibrant, faith-centered student community.
